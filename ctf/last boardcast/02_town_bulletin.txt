LARKSPUR TOWN BULLETIN — MAY 2017 — COMMUNITY NOTICEBOARD

TRAIL WORK COMPLETED

The Alder Run Loop has reopened after spring repairs. The east gate is reached from Juniper Lane. At the first fork, the crescent marker points toward the lower path; the sun marker points toward Quarry Rise.

Three small footbridges cross Alder Run on the lower path. The old visitor map labels each crossing only as “Footbridge.” The town’s works register identifies the plaques by asset number, but that register was not included in this bulletin archive.

The Northstar relay hut is beyond one of the crossings. For trail reports, volunteers were asked to quote the asset number stamped on the back of each plaque rather than copy its name.

Reminder: Quarry Rise is not part of the public loop.
