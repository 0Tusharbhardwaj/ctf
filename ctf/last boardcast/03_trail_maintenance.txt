ALDER RUN VOLUNTEER LOG — EXCERPT
Maintainer: “Moss” (fictional trail volunteer)

2018-09-08
Replaced the crescent marker at the fork. Someone had turned it around; it now points downhill toward the river. The sun marker still points uphill to Quarry Rise.

2018-09-09
Checked the bridge plaques after the storm. Walking downhill from the east gate, the asset numbers are AR-14, AR-09, then AR-22. The relay hut path leaves the main trail immediately after the second crossing. Do not cross the third bridge if you are going to the radio hut.

2019-02-17
Brass plaque marked AR-09 polished and legible again. Small engraved line beneath the name says “for those who keep a light.”
